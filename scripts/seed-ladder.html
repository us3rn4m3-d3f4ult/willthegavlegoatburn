Build an order-book ladder

Spreads a budget across three price levels on each side, so a trade of any size finds depth instead of sweeping a single order. Runs in this browser only — your wallet signs every transaction, no key is typed or stored anywhere.

Connection

Chain 8453 (Base). mainnet.base.org rate-limits hard and can leave waits hanging — publicnode is the default, and you can paste any RPC you trust.

The ladder

Half the budget becomes shares to sell (split 40 / 30 / 30 across the levels, nearest first), half is cash to buy with (a third of it per level). Everything is reversible: Unwind cancels the orders, merges the pairs and withdraws the money.

Orders expire at the market's betting-close time. Unwind cancels your open orders, merges the pairs back to USDC and withdraws the lot — so a seed costs about 30 cents of gas, not the capital.
